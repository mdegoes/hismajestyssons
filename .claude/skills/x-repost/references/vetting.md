# Vetting a non-roster account before amplifying it

Run this on any account that isn't already on `worthy-men.html`. It's a
5-minute judgment, not a background check. When in doubt: `pass`.

## Look at

1. **Identity** — bio, pinned post, linked site. Who are they, what do they claim,
   who vouches for them (does the roster follow / engage them)?
2. **Last ~20 posts** — recurring themes, tone, who they argue with and how. Would
   this account sit comfortably next to Doug Wilson and Michael Foster on the
   roster, or is that a stretch?
3. **The specific post** you'd amplify — is it their exact words, in context, not
   a subtweet or a pile-on?

## Doctrinal red flags (any one → `pass` for direct amplification)

- Prosperity gospel / word-of-faith
- Egalitarianism / women's ordination advocacy
- Revivalist emotionalism as the whole point
- Kinism, ethno-nationalism as identity, race idolatry
- Rome / Eastern-Orthodox apologetics (converts arguing people out of Protestantism)
- Open heresy (denies the Trinity, the resurrection, penal substitution, etc.)
- Obvious grift (endless course funnels, supplement shilling, crypto)

## Conduct red flags (one → `amplify-with-care`; several → `pass`)

- Slop / engagement-bait / AI-filler posting
- Needless cruelty, punching down, mocking the weak
- Doxxing or encouraging it
- Chronic melodrama, main-character energy, manufactured feuds
- Amplifies unverified news / screenshots without links as a habit

## Sanity checks

- Not a bot or a purchased-follower account (engagement roughly tracks follower count).
- Account has history (not two weeks old with 40k followers).
- Real name or a stable, known pseudonym — not a throwaway.

## Verdict

- **`amplify`** — clean on doctrine and conduct; quote-post freely.
- **`amplify-with-care`** — sound but has a conduct wrinkle; only amplify the
  specific strong post, add framing, tell the owner what the wrinkle is.
- **`pass`** — a doctrinal red flag, or several conduct flags. If the underlying
  point is worth making, treat it as *original post inspired by* instead — HMS
  makes the point in its own voice, no amplification.

Record the verdict + one-line reason in the package's `source.md`.
