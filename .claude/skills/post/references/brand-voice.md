## Voice principles

- **Terse and declarative.** Short sentences. Sentence fragments are fine.
  Imperatives are the house style: *Guard the gate. Lead the table. Show up armed.*
- **Verb-first imperative lists, not noun lists.** Draft "Catechize. Read Scripture.
  Lead the table." — not "Catechism. Scripture. The table." The owner rewrites
  noun fragments into commands every time.
- **The hard negation device lands.** *"Not mothers, not pastors, and not the
  state. Fathers."* / *"Not the youth pastor. Not the school. Not the algorithm.
  You."* Name the false substitutes, then the real answer. Offer a variant using it.
- **Em-dashes, not exclamation points.** Gravitas, not hype. One `!` is already too many most days.
- **Concrete over abstract.** "Prayer at the table. Scripture on the shelf." not "prioritize spiritual disciplines."
- **A little dry wit is welcome** (cf. the site's Trust Score food scale). Never smug, never cruel.
- **Scripture is KJV.** "hath", "ye", "thou". Cite book chapter:verse.
- **Roman numerals / `§` marks** read well in long-form on the site — skip them in tweets.
- **First person plural is the brotherhood.** "We" = HMS and the men in it.

## Lexicon

**Reach for:** kingdom · take ground · conscripted · the enemy · brotherhood ·
sworn · castle · household · headship · dominion · Christendom · no neutral ground ·
stand · hold the line · the long view · worthy.

**Avoid:** therapeutic / self-help register ("journey", "showing up for yourself",
"do the work") · "just" as a softener · hedging ("kind of", "I think maybe") ·
evangelical cliché ("do life together", "God-shaped hole", "living my best life") ·
hashtags · engagement-bait ("RT if you agree") · subtweets and drama.

## Theology guardrails

In-bounds: Reformed / confessional, postmillennial optimism, complementarian and
patriarchal, classical Christian education, Christian-nationalism-friendly,
covenantal family life, American Christian history.

Out-of-bounds to amplify: prosperity gospel, egalitarianism, revivalistic
emotionalism as the point, kinism / ethnic idolatry, Rome / Eastern-Orthodox
apologetics, open heresy, grift.

## What NOT to amplify

- Doctrinally-off sources (see guardrails), even when the specific take is fine.
- Unverified news, screenshots with no link, ragebait with no kingdom frame.
- Low-trust hot takes, pure partisan noise, dunk threads, doomerism.
- Anything that punches down or trades in cruelty.

## Credit norms

- **Quote-post beats a screenshot.** If you must screenshot, still link and @-credit.
- Quote a person's **exact words** on the image card. Don't paraphrase into their mouth.
- Attribution format on cards: `— Full Name` then `@handle` on its own line.

## Length

Aim well under 280 characters. A quote-post reads best at 1–2 short sentences of
commentary. Count characters before presenting variants.
