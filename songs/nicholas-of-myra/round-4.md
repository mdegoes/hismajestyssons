[Verse 1: The Great Persecution] (solo, drum like a heartbeat)
Three hundred years from Christ there came
The Diocletian rod:
"Burn ev'ry Scripture! Curse the Name!
And bow to Caesar's god!"
Rome crammed her jails with men of prayer
Till thieves could find no cell,
And Nicholas, in irons there,
Sang psalms in Caesar's hell.

[Chorus] (full choir)
Not the fat man of the fable—
Gold by night and sword flung down,
Fist that crossed the council table:
Christ alone shall wear the crown!
Nicholas of Myra!

[Verse 2: The Three Daughters] (solo, pulled back)
A poor man watched his daughters grow
With not a coin to wed,
And feared the road the poor must go:
To sell themselves for bread.
Then thrice, beneath the cloak of night,
Gold rang upon the floor,
Flung by a hand kept out of sight,
And barred the brothel door.

[Verse 3: The Three Condemned] (men, driving)
Three guiltless men knelt in the sand,
Sold by a bribèd judge;
A sword rose in the headsman's hand,
And not a man would budge.
Nicholas shouldered through the crowd
And tore away the steel;
He flung it down and cried aloud,
"Rise up! You shall not kneel!"

[Chorus]

[Verse 4: The Storm] (full band, choir hums)
Black water rose to meet the sky;
The mast cracked like a bone,
And hardened seamen raised the cry
To gods of wood and stone.
Nicholas gripped the splintered rail
And prayed above the roar;
Then God rebuked the howling gale
And brought them safe to shore.

[Bridge: Nicaea] (building line by line)
In great Nicaea's hall,
"There was when He was not!"
Cried Arius before them all,
Till ev'ry scar grew hot.
The hall in horror froze;
Not one man left his place;
Then Nicholas of Myra rose
And struck him in the face!

[Verse 5: The Fable] (solo, near silence)
Then, full of years, he bowed his head,
The psalm upon his breath:
"Into Your hand"—and he was dead;
But Christ had conquered death.
The ages ground his name to "Claus"
And dressed him up in fur,
And sold him, to the crowd's applause:
A laugh, a sleigh, a blur.

[Final Chorus] (key lift, everyone)
Not the fat man of the fable—
Gold by night and sword flung down,
Fist that crossed the council table:
Christ alone shall wear the crown!
Nicholas of Myra!
Very God of very God!
