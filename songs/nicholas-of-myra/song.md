[Verse 1: The Great Persecution] (solo, drum like a heartbeat)
Three hundred years from Christ there came
The Diocletian rod:
"Burn all the Scriptures! Curse the Name!
And kneel to Jove as god!"
Rome crammed her jails with men of prayer
Till thieves could find no cell,
But Nicholas in irons there
Sang psalms in Caesar's hell.

[Chorus] (full choir)
Not the fat man of the fable,
But a bishop of Myra town.
Iron fist at council table.
Wonders worked to Christ's renown.

[Verse 2: The Three Daughters] (solo, pulled back)
A poor man watched his daughters grow;
No dowry; none could wed.
He saw the street where they must go
To sell themselves for bread.
Then thrice a purse, by dead of night,
Rang out upon the floor,
Flung by a hand kept out of sight,
That barred the brothel door.

[Verse 3: The Three Condemned] (men, driving)
Three guiltless men knelt in the sand,
Sold by a crooked judge;
A sword rose in the headsman's hand,
And not a man would budge.
Nicholas shouldered through the crowd
And tore the blade away;
He cast it down and cried aloud,
"Unbind these three, I say!"

[Chorus]

[Verse 4: The Storm] (full band, choir hums)
Black water rose to meet the sky;
The mast cracked like a bone,
And hardened seamen raised the cry
To gods of wood and stone.
Nicholas gripped the splintered rail
And prayed above the roar;
And God Himself rebuked the gale
And brought them safe to shore.

[Bridge: Nicaea] (building line by line)
Arius, brazen, cried to all,
"There was a time when Christ was not!"
A shudder shook Nicaea hall;
The elders' blood ran cold, then hot.
Then Nicholas of Myra rose
And crossed the floor with measured pace;
He raised the fist that bore Rome's blows,
And struck Arius in the face!

[Verse 5: The Fable] (solo, near silence)
Grown full of years, he bowed his head,
A psalm upon his breath:
"Into thine hand," the old saint said,
Sure Christ had conquered death.
Then ages passed, and men forgot
The fist, the gold, the sword;
There was no time when Christ was not,
Yet time forgot his Lord.

[Final Chorus] (key lift, everyone)
Not the fat man of the fable,
But a bishop of Myra town.
Iron fist at council table.
Wonders worked to Christ's renown.
Iron fist at council table.
Wonders worked to Christ's renown.
