# The Heavens Blaze Jehovah's Name

<!-- meter: verses 8.8.8.8 D iambic ABAB CDCD · chorus 7.7.7.7 D trochaic ABAB · bridge 6.6.6.6 D iambic -->
<!-- source: Psalm 19 (KJV) -->

[Verse 1]
The heavens blaze Jehovah's name;
The firmament displays His skill;
Day speaks to day in tongues of flame,
And night to night is teaching still.
They speak, and yet no voice is heard;
Of all earth's tongues they borrow none;
The world's far shores receive their word,
A sermon preached by stars and sun.

[Verse 2]
The sun, whose tent God pitched on high,
Comes forth, a bridegroom robed and crowned,
A strong man glad to run the sky,
Unwearied on his daily round.
He bears his fire from east to west;
There is no cave where men may hide;
His heat goes searching every breast
And throws the secret places wide.

[Chorus]
Sweeter than the honeycomb,
Worth far more than finest gold,
Firmer than the starry dome,
Is the Word, and it will hold.
Speak, O King, and I will stand;
Draw Your Word, the two-edged sword;
I will march at Your command.
Christ, the Word made flesh, is Lord!

[Verse 3]
His law, more searching still than fire,
Restores the soul and makes it wise;
His statutes are the heart's desire;
His pure commandment lights the eyes.
His fear is clean; His judgments, sure,
Are true and righteous, every one;
And these, like bedrock, shall endure
When sun and stars have had their run.

[Chorus]

[Verse 4]
But who can understand his sin?
My secret faults escape my sight;
Then cleanse me, Lord: from deep within
Drag every hidden thing to light.
Keep back Your servant from the pride
Of willful sin; forbid its reign;
Then, hidden in the Crucified,
I'll stand upright without a stain.

[Bridge]
The sun runs forth in flame;
The Son came down in grace;
He bore my curse and shame
And ran my forfeit race.
The law I could not keep
He kept, and took its blows;
Then, waking from death's sleep,
The righteous Sun arose!

[Verse 5]
Then let the words upon my tongue,
The meditations of my heart,
Ring true as all the stars have sung,
And with the heavens bear their part.
O Rock, my Strength, Redeemer, Lord,
Who bought me back and bore my blame,
Let heart and tongue, with one accord,
Blaze, like the heav'ns, Your holy name!

[Final Chorus]
Sweeter than the honeycomb,
Worth far more than finest gold,
Firmer than the starry dome,
Is the Word, and it will hold.
Reign, O King, o'er sea and land;
Wield Your Word, the two-edged sword;
Earth and heav'n at Your command
Bow and cry that Christ is Lord!
