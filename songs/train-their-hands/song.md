# Train Their Hands for War

[Verse 1: The Shepherd Boy] (unison, steady)
Young David kept his father's sheep
Alone upon the hill;
He struck the lion down to save
The lamb it came to kill.
The God who taught that boy to stand
And face the giant's spear
Now puts the sling into our hands
And bids us not to fear.

[Chorus] (full)
Blessèd be my Strength, the LORD,
Who has trained my hands for war,
Taught my fingers how to fight,
Put our enemies to flight.
Let the giants curse and rail.
Not one word of God shall fail!

[Verse 2: Breath and Shadow] (quiet)
What is a man, that God should know
Or mark his passing breath?
His days like evening shadows grow,
And he goes down to death.
Though these my hands will lose their hold,
And one day my race be run;
The maker is not nor old:
He trains each rising son.

[Chorus]

[Verse 3: Bow Your Heavens] (building, urgent)
Bow low Your heavens, LORD; come down;
Let mountains smoke and quake;
Let lightning strike the giant's crown,
And all his armies break.
Send forth Your hand from heaven and save
From strangers and their boast,
Whose mouths are open as the grave,
Who dare defy Your host.

[Bridge] (near-quiet, building line by line to full)
Heaven bowed when He came,
The Word in flesh and bone;
The Shepherd bore my shame
And faced the cross alone.
The hands that train my hands
Were pierced upon the tree;
He broke death's iron bands:
The devil's prey is free!

[Verse 4: A New Song] (everyone, full; harp or strings enter)
The hands You trained to sling the stone
Now strike the harp's ten strings;
A new song sounds before Your throne,
For You deliver kings.
You turned the giant's hurtful blade
Against its master's head;
So shall Your servants, unafraid,
March on where David led.

[Verse 5: Our Sons] (fathers alone; full voice)
LORD, let our sons like cedars stand,
Unbowed when giants rail;
Our daughters, fairest in the land,
As wise as Abigail.
And when our hands have lost their hold
And we are here no more,
Then be their Strength, as ours of old,
And train their hands for war.

[Chorus] (key lift up a whole step; everyone)

[Tag] (everyone)
Blessed be the people who
To God's name are ever true.
Blessed be those who proclaim,
The Lord is God, and laud His name.
