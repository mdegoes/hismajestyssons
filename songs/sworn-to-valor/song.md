# Sworn to Valor

<!-- meter: verses 8.8.8.8 D iambic ABAB CDCD · chorus 8.7.8.7 / 7.8.7.8 trochaic -->
<!-- source: the owner's six-line warrior's code (adapted from the "Old Code" in Dragonheart, 1996) -->

[Verse 1: Valor]
The warrior stands, to valor sworn;
He will not flinch, he will not flee;
He holds the breach where walls are torn
When none but God is there to see.
He fears the Lord, and so fears none,
Not even death, for death is gain;
He fights until the fight is done,
And if he falls, he falls to reign.

[Verse 2: Virtue]
His heart knows virtue through and through;
He binds his eyes by solemn vow,
And flees the lusts that men pursue,
And keeps his hand upon the plow.
His heart's a city, walled and barred,
With Christ the King enthroned within;
He stands upon the walls on guard
And opens not the gate to sin.

[Chorus]
Sworn to valor, pure in virtue,
Shield the helpless, lift the weak;
Speak the truth though it should hurt you;
Strike the wolf that stalks the meek.
Hold the line and never yield;
Never shall your God forsake you:
He, your sword; and He, your shield.
Keep the code till death shall take you.

[Verse 3: Defending the Helpless]
His strength defends the helpless poor,
The widow and the fatherless;
He bars the robber from their door
And pleads their cause, and wins redress.
Where innocents are dragged to die,
He will not say, "I did not know";
He will not turn and pass them by,
But throws himself before the blow.

[Verse 4: Upholding the Weak]
His might upholds the weak man's hands
When aged and weary arms give way;
Like Aaron at his side he stands,
And holds them up till end of day.
He will not break the bruisèd reed,
Nor snuff the wick that faintly burns;
He lifts the fallen man in need,
And walks with him till strength returns.

[Chorus]

[Verse 5: Truth]
His word speaks only what is true;
His yea is yea, his nay is nay;
The oath he swears, he carries through,
Though heavy be the price to pay.
He takes no bribe, nor turns aside;
He'd sooner wound a friend than lie;
Though he must stand against the tide,
He looks the mighty in the eye.

[Verse 6: Wrath]
His wrath undoes the wicked man,
But not for any private wrong;
He breaks his dark and crooked plan,
And takes the prey back from the strong.
He's slow to wrath and swift to hear,
But does not bear the sword in vain.
With holy fury, rev'rent fear,
He wars until the dragon's slain.

[Chorus]
