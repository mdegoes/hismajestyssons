"Octave up" helps on laptop speakers, which drop the low notes. Tempo is in dotted-quarter beats per minute. Syllables and stresses are guessed from spelling; fix a miscount by hyphenating the word. Chords are a simple sketch for judging the tune, not a finished arrangement.
